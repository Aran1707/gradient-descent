# Sources and Further Reading

These are included for your own reference; do not overload the beginner part of the seminar with papers.

- PyTorch optimizer documentation: https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/optim.html
- Lion paper: Xiangning Chen et al., “Symbolic Discovery of Optimization Algorithms”, arXiv:2302.06675
- SAM paper: Pierre Foret et al., “Sharpness-Aware Minimization for Efficiently Improving Generalization”, arXiv:2010.01412
- AdamW paper: Ilya Loshchilov and Frank Hutter, “Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization”, arXiv:1711.05101
- MNIST archive used by uploaded code: https://storage.googleapis.com/tensorflow/tf-keras-datasets/mnist.npz
